# AI Research Prompts

A collection of prompt templates for using AI assistants (LLMs) at various stages of the research process — literature review, methodology design, hypothesis development, peer review prep, and source verification.

---

## 1. The "Asymmetric-Yield" Literature Synthesizer

> Act as an Elite Academic Researcher. I need to master the research landscape of [Topic] in 45 minutes. Conduct a rapid structural sprint to isolate the 3 most influential alternative foundational papers and the 3 most critical conflicting arguments. Deliver a strict, high-contrast text validation layout mapping these intellectual battlegrounds where current literature consensus fails.

---

## 2. The "Prestige-Identity" Methodology Blueprint

> Act as a Senior Research Methodologist. Help me build an alternative, rigorous research design framework for analyzing complex datasets. Format this framework using a strict "Immediate Epistemological Hook + High-Contrast Variables Mapping + Single Low-Friction Data Validation Protocol" visual layout explicitly engineered to secure absolute experimental integrity under rapid execution.

---

## 4. The "Humanized-Velocity" Hypothesis Builder

> Act as an Elite Epistemologist and Academic Coach. Design an alternative, text-based, zero-fluff workspace template engineered to construct, stress-test, and refine high-yielding research hypotheses. Show me exactly how to structure an organic, non-robotic analytical narrative within the prompt rules, keeping explanations deeply grounded to bypass dense academic jargon and isolate core truth.

---

## 5. The "Hyper-Targeted" Counter-Argument Parser

> Act as an Adversarial Peer Reviewer. Write 3 alternative, high-impact analytical deconstruction layouts designed to stress-test the validity of my core research arguments. Every layout must utilize a sharp, high-contrast text pattern-interrupt framework engineered to expose hidden assumptions, highlight data collection flaws, and challenge the statistical significance of primary qualitative findings.

---

## 6. The "Two-Step" Source Verification Pipeline

> Act as a Master Fact-Checker and Research Librarian. Write an alternative, actionable visual layout blueprint and custom evaluation protocol to verify the authority of any paper or dataset in my niche. Structure a strict "Two-Step" framework: first, define an immediate, low-friction citation-crosscheck to isolate conflicts of interest; second, close with a fast-converting, clean bias checklist.

---

## 7. The "Skeptic-Filter" Peer Review Shield

> Act as a Senior Journal Editor. Create an alternative, repeatable, text-based validation prompt framework designed to act as a defensive shield for my draft manuscript against brutal academic critiques. Show me exactly how to map out a clear, high-contrast defense matrix that preempts objections regarding sample size, variable controls, or technical implementation gaps to guarantee publication.

---

## 8. The "Velocity-Check" Research Progress Audit

> Act as a Data-Driven Research Project Manager. My research output and analysis metrics over the last 14 days are: [Sources Analyzed] volume, [Hypotheses Validated] velocity, [Synthesis Draft Completion]%, and [Data Integrity Score] metrics. Diagnose the exact alternative structural formatting flaw, cognitive bias mismatch, or literature sourcing bottleneck stalling my accelerated research thesis.
