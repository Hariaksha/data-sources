## Notes & Quirks

This source is **not** a single downloadable dataset. It is primarily an interactive storytelling and visualization layer built on top of World Bank data products.[1][2]

The Atlas is useful for discovering indicators, framing questions, and identifying themes, but researchers who need raw downloadable data should use the linked Data360 data resources, API, World Development Indicators tools, DataBank, or the World Bank Data Catalog.[2][3]

Coverage, unit of observation, and download options vary depending on the underlying indicator rather than the Atlas itself.[1][2]

***

## How to Access

1. Start at the [Atlas homepage](https://data360.worldbank.org/en/atlas/) to explore themes and stories.[1]
2. Use the Atlas to identify relevant indicators or development topics.[1]
3. Move to Data360’s [About](https://data360.worldbank.org/en/about) information and related API/data tools to locate downloadable underlying data.[2]
4. For bulk downloads or panel-ready sources, check the World Bank [Open Data portal](https://data.worldbank.org), Data Catalog, DataBank, or the Microdata Library as appropriate.[3]
