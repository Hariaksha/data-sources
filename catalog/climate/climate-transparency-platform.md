# Climate Transparency Platform

**Theme:** Climate Policy / Environmental Governance / Capacity Building
**Coverage:** Global; country pages, projects, agencies, experts, knowledge products, and UNFCCC transparency support resources
**Unit of observation:** Country / Project / Agency / Expert / Knowledge product
**Temporal granularity:** Mixed; project-based and document-based, with ongoing updates
**Format:** Web portal
**Access:** https://climate-transparency-platform.org/ — free; some features require account registration
**License:** Not clearly stated on homepage; verify terms before reuse
**Last verified:** June 2026

---

## Key Variables

| Variable | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Country profile | Country-level transparency support information and related activities |
| CBIT projects | Capacity-Building Initiative for Transparency projects by country and status |
| GEF enabling activities | Country support activities related to transparency and reporting |
| UNFCCC reporting resources | Reporting-related materials and country-linked transparency information |
| Agencies | Implementing or supporting agencies active on the platform |
| Experts | Listed technical experts connected to transparency support |
| Knowledge products | Guidelines, tools, publications, and other technical resources |
| News and events | Webinars, calls, exchanges, and other platform updates |

## Notes & Quirks

- The platform is centered on climate transparency support under the UNFCCC ecosystem rather than serving as a raw statistical dataset.
- It includes navigation for countries, projects, agencies, experts, news and events, and a knowledge centre, which makes it useful as a structured metadata source rather than a conventional downloadable database.
- The homepage highlights transparency overview content, CBIT projects, GEF enabling activities, UNFCCC reporting, and networks as key entry points.
- Public access is available through the main site, but account features such as login and registration are also present.
- Because this is a dynamic portal, coverage and available project entries can change over time; any reproducible research use should record scrape dates carefully.
- Before reusing documents or extracted material, check the site terms and conditions or page-specific licensing.

## How to Access

1. Start at the homepage: [https://climate-transparency-platform.org/](https://climate-transparency-platform.org/)
2. Use the **Countries** section for country-linked transparency support records.
3. Use **Projects** to review active and past platform-linked initiatives, especially CBIT-related work.
4. Use **Agencies** and **Experts** to identify implementing partners and technical support actors.
5. Use the **Knowledge Centre** for guidelines, tools, and publications relevant to transparency reporting.
6. Record access dates when citing platform content because entries are updated over time.
