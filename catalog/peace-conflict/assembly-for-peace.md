# Assembly for Peace

**Theme:** Peace & Conflict / International Institutions (UN General Assembly practice)
**Source:** United Nations University Centre for Policy Research (UNU-CPR); updates for the 79th and 80th GA sessions reviewed with the Office of the President of the General Assembly (80th session)
**Coverage:** UN General Assembly action on peace and security, 1950–present (about seven decades), including cases such as Korea, the Suez Crisis, Cambodia, and Syria
**Unit of observation:** Case study / GA action (qualitative), plus summary tables and a timeline
**Temporal granularity:** Case-level; platform updated annually (per GA resolution 79/327)
**Format:** Interactive web platform (searchable handbook, case studies, timeline, summary charts, resource library); no structured data download
**Access:** [https://assemblyforpeace.unu.edu](https://assemblyforpeace.unu.edu) — free, no registration
**License:** CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO (attribution, non-commercial, share-alike)
**Last verified:** September 2026

---

## What It Is

Assembly for Peace documents how the UN General Assembly has used its powers on peace and security, especially where the Security Council is deadlocked. It launched on September 8, 2026 as an interactive version of UNU-CPR's 2024 PDF handbook, *Assembly for Peace: A Digital Handbook*, updated with material from the 79th and 80th GA sessions and expanded case coverage.

It is a **qualitative reference resource**, not a quantitative dataset. It's most useful for identifying and coding GA interventions by hand, or for background on institutional mechanisms.

---

## Key Content

- **65+ case studies** of GA action on peace and security
- **Eight thematic practice areas**, including peace operations, responses to the use of force, sanctions, accountability mechanisms, mediation, and humanitarian action
- **12+ summary charts/reference tables**, including all debates held under the **Veto Initiative** (GA resolution 76/262, which requires the GA to meet after a Security Council veto)
- **UN Charter basis** for GA engagement on peace and security, and comparisons of GA vs. Security Council roles
- **Interactive timeline** of major GA interventions
- **Resource library** linking further research and learning materials

## Potential Research Questions

- When the Security Council is deadlocked (e.g., Veto Initiative debates), does GA action change conflict dynamics measured in [ACLED](acled.md) or [GDELT](gdelt.md) event data?
- Which types of GA action (sanctions recommendations, accountability mechanisms, peace operations) come before measurable changes in conflict intensity or [Global Peace Index](global-peace-index.md) scores?
- Can the case studies be hand-coded into a GA-intervention event dataset (country, year, action type) to merge with conflict panels?
- Has GA peace-and-security activity increased since the 2022 Veto Initiative, and in which regions?

## Notes & Quirks

- **Not a structured dataset.** There is no CSV/API export; turning cases into variables means manual coding from the case pages and summary tables.
- **Curated selection of cases.** The 65+ cases illustrate GA practice rather than exhaustively listing every resolution or vote. For complete voting records, use UN Digital Library voting data instead.
- **Non-commercial license.** CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO allows research reuse with attribution, but derived works must be shared under the same license and not used commercially.
- **Annual updates.** Content reflects GA sessions through the 80th; later sessions are added annually, so check for newer material before citing coverage end dates.
